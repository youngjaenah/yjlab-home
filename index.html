lunashot

A planner for capturing the moon or sun rising over a landmark — it calculates when and where to shoot, in one place.

  • One tool instead of juggling maps, ephemeris apps, and spreadsheets
  • Direction and timing of moonrise and moonset past your landmark
  • Finds the best shooting spots to line up the moon or sun with your target
