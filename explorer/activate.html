Explorer Pro

Get your license file

Paste the license key from your purchase email. You'll get a small license file that unlocks Pro in Explorer — it works offline, forever, on your Macs.

You can come back and download it again any time with the same key.

Then, in Explorer

  1. Double-click the downloaded Explorer-Pro.explorerlicense file, or
  2. open Explorer ▸ Settings ▸ Explorer Pro and choose Import License… (you can also paste the file's contents there).
