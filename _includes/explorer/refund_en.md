# Explorer Pro Refund Policy

**Effective date: 2026-10-01**
**Last updated: 2026-10-01**

You can try Explorer for Mac as long as you like with the free version before buying Pro. If Pro still isn't right for you, we'll refund it as described below.

## 1. Eligibility

- **Within 14 days of purchase**, we give a **full refund**, no questions asked.
- After 14 days, we still refund billing errors, duplicate charges, and cases where a Pro feature does not work and we are unable to fix it.
- Where applicable consumer law gives you stronger rights, that law applies.

## 2. How to request a refund

Use whichever is easier:

1. **Email** support@yjlab.io with your order number (or the email address you purchased with).
2. **Lemon Squeezy**: request it from your purchase confirmation email or your Lemon Squeezy order page.

## 3. Processing

1. Payments are handled by **Lemon Squeezy**, our Merchant of Record, so refunds are issued through Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method.
2. Refunds are usually processed within a few business days of the request; depending on your bank or payment method, it can take 5–10 business days to appear.

## 4. Your license after a refund

Once a refund is complete, the license key is deactivated, the license file can no longer be downloaded, and the right to use the license ends. Please remove it in the App under **Settings ▸ Explorer Pro ▸ Remove License**. All free features keep working as before.
